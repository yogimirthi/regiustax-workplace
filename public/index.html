Laptop Shutdown & Sync Protection
  • Before Shutdown: Click "Take Backup Now" or double-click take_backup.bat on your laptop.
  • After Reboot: Simply double-click start_rtwhatsup.bat. All your data is instantly preserved.
  • Sync to Cloud/USB: Run sync_to_usb_or_cloud.bat to mirror the backups\ folder to Google Drive, OneDrive, or a USB pen drive.
  • Missed Backup Protection: If your laptop was off at 12:00 PM, the system automatically runs a catch-up backup as soon as it starts!
